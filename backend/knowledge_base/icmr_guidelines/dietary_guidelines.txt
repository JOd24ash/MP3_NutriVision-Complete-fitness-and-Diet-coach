Title: Dietary Guidelines for Indians, ICMR-NIN (2024)
Source: https://www.nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjs/locale/DGI07052024P.pdf

Summary prepared for NutriVision. ICMR-NIN dietary guidance emphasizes varied diets built from minimally processed foods, vegetables and fruits, pulses or other protein foods, and appropriate portions. Individual medical conditions and allergies require personalised professional advice. This summary does not replace medical advice.
